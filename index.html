05 / About

A perspective shaped by two landscapes.

Studio Thames is led by Massaquoi Thames, a photographer, filmmaker, and drone pilot drawn to the quiet power of wild places.

With roots in West Africa and a creative life shaped in the American West, Massaquoi brings a perspective centered on connection, scale, and honest observation. His work helps brands and destinations show not only what a place or product looks like, but what it feels like to be there.
